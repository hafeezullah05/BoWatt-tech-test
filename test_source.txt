BoWatt Case Study Internal Notes

Project Zephyrine-9 is an internal codename used by the fictional company Marlowtide Systems for their experimental battery recycling process. The process was developed by lead engineer Priya Vantham in the year 2031.

The Zephyrine-9 process achieves a battery material recovery rate of 94.7 percent, which is significantly higher than the industry average of around 65 percent at the time.

Marlowtide Systems is headquartered in a fictional city called Ashcombe Bay. The company's mascot is a cartoon octopus named Ozzie.

The Zephyrine-9 process uses a proprietary solvent called Tridexolyne-C, which was accidentally discovered by a junior lab technician named Farid Osei during an unrelated experiment in 2029.
